A voice-only keyboard: tap the microphone, speak, and the text appears in whatever app you are typing in.

Speech is recognized on the phone by tinyasr with its en-ru-uk model. The app has no internet permission, so audio never leaves the device.

* Live text while you speak, with punctuation and capitalization
* Language key for Ukrainian, English, Russian, or automatic detection among the ones you choose
* Keys for delete, enter, space and basic punctuation; one tap back to your normal keyboard
